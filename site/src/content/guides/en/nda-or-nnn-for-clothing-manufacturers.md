## Short answer

Not an NDA alone, and not a US-drafted one. A Delaware NDA signed by a Guangzhou garment factory is paper you can wave in a courtroom where that factory has no assets and no registered presence. The instrument with teeth inside the PRC is an NNN agreement — Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention — written under Chinese law, executed in Chinese, against the factory's business-licence name, carrying a stated liquidated-damages figure and naming a Guangzhou people's court as venue. Even that is the second line of defence. The first is operational: never let one factory hold your design, your cost sheet and your buyer list at the same time.

## NDA vs NNN: what each document actually stops

| Instrument | What it stops | Enforceable in a PRC court | Cost in Guangzhou | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US-style NDA | Proving they repeated your spec | Rarely: wrong law, wrong venue, no local entity | USD 0-200 template | Decorative |
| NNN under PRC law, Chinese text | Disclosure, reuse of your design, direct contact with your buyers | Yes, if licence entity, damages and venue are named | RMB 3,000-6,000, 3-5 working days | The baseline |
| Copyright deposit of artwork | Look-alike clones of your print | Yes, strongest evidentiary weight | RMB 300-1,200 plus agent fee, days to file | Cheap, do it always |
| Design patent | A copyist reproducing your silhouette | Yes, but only after grant | RMB 4,000-9,000, 4-10 months | Hero SKUs only |
| Split production | Nobody ever holding the full cost structure | Self-enforcing, no court needed | Zero cash, one extra freight leg | Highest return |
| Tooling ownership clause | Reuse of your screens, blocks, molds, laser plates | Yes, if stated item by item | Zero | Add one paragraph |

The two clauses people cut for brevity are the two that get used. Non-Use stops a contractor running your silhouette on its own line for the domestic wholesale market while your order sits in its queue. Non-Circumvention stops it quoting your buyers 30% under you. A disclosure-only clause blocks neither, and both of those are what actually happen.

## Paper the relationship in six moves

1. Verify the entity before disclosing anything. Confirm the business-licence name matches the name signing the agreement and the name on the invoice. A trading company signing for a 40-person sewing workshop in Panyu binds nobody, because the workshop never signed.
2. Contract through a party that can stand in court: your own PRC-registered entity, or a licensed Chinese firm holding your power of attorney.
3. Have a PRC-licensed drafter write the NNN in Chinese. It must fix governing law (PRC), venue (a named Guangzhou people's court), the definition of confidential material (tech pack, patterns, wash formulas, trim sourcing list, buyer list, price sheet), liquidated damages of RMB 100,000-300,000 per breach, and a survival term of three years past the last order.
4. Version every disclosure. Each revision of the tech pack for manufacturer review leaves your hands as a date-stamped PDF with a file hash, logged against the recipient's name and date. No trail means no proof of what they received.
5. Split the chain. Knit or buy fabric through your own account, ship trims separately, let the sewing shop see only construction. A Guangzhou streetwear manufacturer that never saw your fabric cost sheet cannot quote your buyer convincingly.
6. Make the relationship worth more than the theft. Concentrate volume in one or two partners, pay balances on schedule, show them a reorder pipeline. Shops expecting next season's order rarely burn this season's client.

## What copying looks like on the ground

Among clothing manufacturers in Guangzhou China, design loss is mostly not theft — it is speed. Walk a stock hoodie into the Shisanhang wholesale blocks and a 320 GSM brushed-fleece copy reaches a stall in the Hongbiantian building within 7 to 12 days at RMB 38-58 per piece, against RMB 72-88 for a comparable private label hoodie manufacturer build at 300 pieces per colour. Shahe runs cheaper again at RMB 25-40 and is where leaked stock gets liquidated, which tells you the margin on a leaked order is real. Zhanxi is likely where your own contractor sits: CMT of RMB 38-55 per hoodie, 100 pieces per colour minimum on in-stock fabric, 300 per colour once fabric must be knitted and dyed, with circular-knit greige dye lots opening at 300 kg per colour. A swatch you hand over is reproducible from a neighbouring Zhongda counter inside 48 hours, at which point your confidential fabric is public regardless of what the NNN says.

## Inspection beats litigation as a contract clause

Put the enforceable language in the purchase order, not the NDA. Write quality control china manufacturing terms into the PO: AQL 2.5 major and 4.0 minor at inspection level II, which for a 5,000-piece lot means a 200-piece sample, accept at 10 majors, reject at 11. Hold 70% of payment against a passed report. China quality control inspection services cost RMB 1,800-2,200 per inspector-day locally, USD 250-300 through an international firm, report inside 24 hours. Nobody sues over 300 pieces, but a factory that knows it will not be paid until a stranger counts seams behaves differently from one that knows you hold only a document.
