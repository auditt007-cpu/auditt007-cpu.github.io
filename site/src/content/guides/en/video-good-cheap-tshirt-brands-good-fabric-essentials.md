Sourcing good quality, affordable t-shirts for essentials requires a clear understanding of fabric specifications, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and quality control (QC) standards in Guangzhou. While there isn't one single "brand" to recommend for cheap essentials, the focus should be on factory capabilities and fabric consistency.

## Sourcing Essentials: Practical Steps

1.  **Define Fabric Specifications:** Specify your desired fabric composition (e.g., 100% cotton, CVC, TC) and target fabric GSM. For essential t-shirts, a good fabric GSM for 100% cotton is typically between 180-220 GSM. This range offers a balance of durability and comfort without being overly heavy or sheer. For performance blends, the fabric GSM might vary.
2.  **Establish MOQ Requirements:** Be realistic about your MOQ. For basic t-shirts, factories in Guangzhou, particularly those supplying markets like Shahe (沙河), generally require 300-500 pieces per style per color. Some larger, more established factories might have MOQs of 1000 pieces or more.
3.  **Budget Unit Price:** Based on your fabric GSM and MOQ, expect unit prices for basic 180-200 GSM cotton t-shirts to range from $2.50 to $4.00 USD per piece, FOB Guangzhou. Prices will increase for higher fabric GSM, specialized finishes, or lower MOQs.
4.  **Implement QC Protocols:** Mandate AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) standards. A common standard for apparel is AQL 2.5 for major defects (e.g., holes, broken stitches, incorrect sizing) and AQL 4.0 for minor defects (e.g., loose threads, small stains). Ensure pre-production samples, in-line inspections, and final random inspections are part of the process.
5.  **Request Samples and Lab Dips:** Always request pre-production samples for approval of fit, construction, and fabric hand-feel. For custom colors, demand lab dips against your Pantone TCX codes to ensure color accuracy before bulk production.
6.  **Consider Factory Location:** Markets like Shisanhang (十三行) and Shahe (沙河) are known for high-volume, competitive pricing on basic apparel. Factories supplying these markets are often well-versed in producing essential t-shirts efficiently.

## Understanding Fabric and Production

When discussing "good fabric" for essentials, the primary metric is fabric GSM (grams per square meter). A higher fabric GSM generally indicates a denser, more durable fabric. For basic cotton t-shirts, 180-220 GSM is a common sweet spot. Below 160 GSM, the fabric can feel thin and less durable. Above 240 GSM, it becomes heavier, more suitable for colder weather or specific streetwear styles.

Many factories in Guangzhou specialize in producing blanks for various brands, including local Chinese streetwear brands. These factories have established supply chains for consistent fabric quality. When sourcing, always request fabric swatches with specified fabric GSM and composition. For example, if you need a heavier feel, you might specify 220 GSM 100% combed cotton. If you're looking for a specific texture, like a slub jersey, ensure the factory can consistently produce that.

Quality control is paramount. Beyond AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor, ensure factories have processes for shrinkage testing (typically within 3-5% for cotton), colorfastness, and pilling resistance. These tests are crucial for essential items that will be washed frequently.
