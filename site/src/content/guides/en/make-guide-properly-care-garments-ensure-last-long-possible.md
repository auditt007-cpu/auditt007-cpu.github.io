Maintaining garment quality extends product life and enhances brand reputation. Proper care instructions are not just a suggestion; they are a critical component of the product's value proposition. As a sourcing agent in Guangzhou, we see firsthand how initial fabric quality and subsequent care impact garment longevity.

## Practical Garment Care Steps

Implementing these steps will significantly prolong the life of apparel, reducing returns and improving customer satisfaction.

1.  **Read Care Labels:** Always check the care label inside the garment. These instructions are specific to the fabric composition and construction. Ignoring them is the fastest way to damage an item.
2.  **Wash Cold and Gentle:** For most garments, especially those with delicate prints or blends, washing in cold water on a gentle cycle is recommended. This minimizes color fading, shrinkage, and fabric stress.
3.  **Air Dry When Possible:** High heat from tumble dryers can degrade elastic fibers, cause shrinkage, and damage embellishments. Air drying, either flat or on a hanger, is often the best option.
4.  **Sort by Color and Fabric Type:** Washing darks with lights can lead to color transfer. Similarly, separating heavy items like denim from delicate silks prevents abrasion and pilling.
5.  **Spot Clean Promptly:** Addressing stains immediately with a targeted spot cleaner can prevent them from setting, avoiding the need for harsher full washes.
6.  **Store Properly:** Fold knitwear to prevent stretching and hang woven items. Use appropriate hangers to maintain garment shape. Avoid overcrowding closets, which can lead to wrinkles and fabric stress.

## Sourcing Realities and Quality Control

The foundation of a long-lasting garment is laid during sourcing and manufacturing. In Guangzhou, markets like Shisanhang (十三行) offer a vast array of fabric options, from lightweight 120 GSM cotton jerseys to heavy 350 GSM fleece. Understanding the fabric gsm guide is crucial for durability. For instance, a basic T-shirt might use a 160 GSM single jersey, while a quality hoodie requires 300-320 GSM French terry.

When we develop a tech pack guide for a new style, such as a tech pack long sleeve, we specify not only the fabric composition and weight but also critical construction details that impact durability. This includes stitch per inch (SPI), seam allowances, and reinforcement points.

## Manufacturing Standards and MOQs

For custom apparel orders, the typical Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 300 pieces per style per color. This MOQ allows factories to achieve economies of scale, offering unit prices ranging from $3.50 for a basic cotton tee (160 GSM) to $12.00 for a more complex woven shirt (220 GSM). For specialized items like a tech pack long sleeve with intricate details, prices can go up to $25.00 per piece at this MOQ.

Quality control is paramount. Our process includes multiple checkpoints:
*   **Fabric Inspection:** Before cutting, ensuring fabric meets specified GSM, color, and defect standards.
*   **In-line Production Checks:** Monitoring stitching, measurements, and print/embroidery application.
*   **Final Random Inspection (FRI):** AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects is applied to the finished goods before shipment. This ensures that the garments leaving the factory meet the buyer's quality expectations, contributing to their overall lifespan.
