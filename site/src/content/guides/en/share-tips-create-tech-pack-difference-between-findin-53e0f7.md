Creating a comprehensive tech pack is essential for effective communication with manufacturers in Guangzhou. Without a clear tech pack, you risk misinterpretations, production delays, and quality issues. The difference between finding a manufacturer and using print-on-demand (POD) lies primarily in control, customization, and scale.

## Creating a Tech Pack for Manufacturers

A tech pack serves as the blueprint for your garment. It provides all necessary information for a factory to understand your design, source materials, and produce your product accurately. This is your primary tool for ensuring quality and consistency.

### Practical Steps to Create a Tech Pack

1.  **Cover Page:** Include your brand logo, style name, style number, season, and date. This provides immediate context for the manufacturer.
2.  **Design Sketch (Front & Back):** Provide clear, flat sketches of your garment from the front and back. Include any visible details like stitching, pockets, or closures.
3.  **Detailed Callouts:** Annotate your sketches with specific details. For example, indicate stitch types (e.g., single needle, double needle), pocket dimensions, and button placements.
4.  **Bill of Materials (BOM):** List every component of your garment. This includes fabric (type, composition, weight like 220 GSM single jersey), trims (buttons, zippers, labels), and any embellishments. Specify colors using Pantone TPX codes.
5.  **Colorways:** For each style, provide separate pages detailing all approved color combinations. Include Pantone codes for each color.
6.  **Size Chart & Grading:** Provide precise measurements for each key point of the garment (e.g., chest width, body length, sleeve length) for your sample size. Include grading rules for all other sizes.
7.  **Construction Details:** Explain how the garment is assembled. This can include diagrams for complex areas like collars, cuffs, or plackets.
8.  **Print/Embroidery Artwork:** If applicable, include vector files for any prints or embroidery, specifying placement, size, and color references.
9.  **Labeling & Packaging Instructions:** Detail all required labels (main label, care label, size label) and their placement. Specify packaging requirements, such as individual polybags, hangtags, and carton markings.

A well-structured tech pack for manufacturer ensures that your vision is translated accurately into a physical product. For instance, when sourcing basic knitwear in Shahe market, a complete tech pack helps factories quote accurately and avoid costly sampling errors.

## Manufacturer vs. Print-on-Demand

The choice between a traditional manufacturer and print-on-demand depends on your business model, desired level of customization, and production volume.

### Pitfall Comparison: Manufacturer vs. Print-on-Demand

| Feature             | Traditional Manufacturer (Guangzhou) | Print-on-Demand (Online Platform) |
| :------------------ | :----------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- |
| Minimum Order Qty   | 300 pieces/style/color               | 1 piece                           |
| Customization Level | High (fabric, pattern, construction) | Low (pre-made blanks, graphic)    |
| Unit Cost (T-shirt) | $3.50 - $7.00 USD (at 300 MOQ)       | $15.00 - $30.00 USD               |
| Lead Time           | 30-60 days                           | 2-7 days                          |
| Quality Control     | AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor            | Basic (platform standard)         |
| Fabric Options      | Thousands (e.g., 180 GSM cotton)     | Limited (standard blanks)         |

When working with a manufacturer in Guangzhou, especially in markets like Shisanhang for women's fashion, you gain full control over fabric selection (e.g., 120 GSM chiffon, 340 GSM denim), garment construction, and quality. This allows for unique product development. The initial investment is higher due to MOQs, but the unit cost is significantly lower, leading to better profit margins at scale. QC checkpoints are typically implemented at fabric inspection, during cutting, and before packing, adhering to international standards like AQL 2.5 major / 4.0 minor.

Print-on-demand services, conversely, offer zero MOQ and no upfront inventory costs. You upload your design, and the POD company prints it on a pre-existing blank garment and ships it directly to your customer. This model is ideal for testing designs, dropshipping, or small-batch custom merchandise. However, customization is limited to graphic application, fabric choices are restricted, and unit costs are substantially higher. The quality control is managed by the POD provider, often with less stringent standards than a dedicated apparel manufacturer. If you are looking for a true "07 tech pack" experience with full control over your product, a manufacturer is the only viable option.

## Conclusion

For serious apparel brands aiming for unique products, competitive pricing, and scalable production, understanding how to create a tech pack and engaging with a manufacturer is crucial. Print-on-demand serves a different niche, primarily for low-volume, graphic-focused merchandise. Your choice should align with your brand's long-term strategy and product vision.
