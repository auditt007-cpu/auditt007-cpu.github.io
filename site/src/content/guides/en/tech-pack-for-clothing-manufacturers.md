## The answer to the question

A video is the wrong format for this. What you need is a fillable field list and knowledge of what a factory reads before it can name a number. Below is the pack I send so clothing manufacturers in Guangzhou China cut, sample and cost without one clarifying message. It is written for a heavyweight hoodie because that is where small brands start; the fields transfer to tees, caps and sweats.

## What a pack tier actually buys you

| Pack tier | What the factory can decide from it | Sample rounds to approval | Quote accuracy | What it costs you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mood board plus reference photo | Nothing; they guess your fit | 4-6 | ±35% on unit price | RMB 1,500-3,000 dead sample fees |
| Flat sketch plus a fabric name | Rough style, no fit, no construction | 3-4 | ±20% | RMB 900-1,800 |
| Full pack: BOM, POM, tolerances, stitch, artwork | They cut a proto unprompted | 1-2 | ±8% | RMB 250-500 first sample, refunded at 300 pieces |
| Full pack plus graded nest and marker plan | Fabric yield costed to the metre | 1 | ±4% | 2-4 days pattern work, no cash |
| Full pack plus approved lab dips and PP sample | Bulk fabric buyable on your sign-off | 0-1 | ±3% | RMB 80-150 per print colour, screens |

The best tech pack template removes questions; it does not add pages. Fourteen slides of brand story with no tolerance column are worth less than two pages carrying every measurement, stitch class and photographed trim.

## Build it in this order

1. Style header on one page: style name, internal code, gender and age group, season, on-sale date, destination market, target landed cost per piece. Factories price to a target when you give them one; otherwise they price to their own margin.
2. Front, back and sleeve flats with numbered callouts at each construction detail; 12-16 callouts leaves no interpretation gap.
3. BOM table, one row per item: name, specification, supplier, consumption, unit, tolerance. For fabric state GSM plus or minus 5%, composition, knit construction (single jersey, interlock, loopback, brushed fleece), yarn count and finish (enzyme wash, silicone, compacted, pre-shrunk).
4. Points of measure: 22-28 on a hoodie, each with how it is measured and its tolerance. Chest 1 cm below the armhole ±0.5 cm, body length from high point of shoulder ±0.6 cm, sleeve from shoulder seam ±0.5 cm, hood height ±0.8 cm.
5. Grade rules as increments, not a size chart alone: hoodie chest 4 cm between sizes, body length 2 cm, sleeve 1 cm; tee chest 5 cm. Name the base size being graded.
6. Construction spec in stitch classes: 504 four-thread overlock at 12-13 SPI, 605 safety seam at 12 SPI, 401 lockstitch at 13-15 SPI, 406 coverstitch at 12-13 SPI. Give seam allowances (1 cm overlock, 1.5 cm safety), shoulder tape, rib weight at cuff and hem.
7. Artwork as vector PDF at 1:1 scale, text outlined, spot colours separated, Pantone TPG references written next to each, placement measured from centre front and high point of shoulder. Name the technique and its constraint: discharge needs 100% cotton, DTG needs pretreat above 50% polyester, chest embroidery capped at 12,000 stitches.
8. Colourways and lab dips: three dips per colour, approved under D65 light at delta E ≤ 1.5, signed before knitting. State the fastness floor: grey scale 4 dry, 3 wet minimum for crocking and wash.
9. Trims and labels, each photographed with spec: 140 cm drawcord, 5 mm eyelet ID, auto-lock zipper with tape 1 cm above the top stop, care label wording, hangtag with EAN-13 barcode.
10. Packing and sample protocol: 20 pieces per poly bag, 60 per carton, carton under 18 kg, plus the milestone ladder named with days allowed: proto, fit, salesman sample, pre-production, ship-with.

## The Guangzhou numbers that belong inside the pack

Order gsm fabric samples from Zhongda before committing: stock greige moves in 2 days, custom dye in 5-7, and 300 kg per colour is the door price at a circular knitting mill. A 380 GSM loopback runs RMB 22-28 per kg and one hoodie consumes 0.55-0.7 kg, so fabric alone is RMB 13-19 per piece before cutting loss. State cuttable width and an 8-12% loss allowance, or consumption becomes an argument on the invoice. A proto costs RMB 250-500 per hoodie, 5-7 working days once the pattern exists and 3-4 with a graded nest supplied. Bulk of 1,000 pieces runs 18-25 working days from an approved PP sample, plus 4-6 for garment enzyme and silicone washing. Shisanhang stock hoodies at RMB 38-58 are what you get from a clothing manufacturer with low moq and no tech pack at all; the same garment built to your pack on a Zhanxi CMT line lands at RMB 72-88 all-in at 300 pieces per colour. Specify all 4 types of quality inspection in the pack: raw fabric, in-line at sewing, pre-shipment at AQL 2.5 major and 4.0 minor, post-shipment review of returns. At a lot of 800, level II is an 80-piece sample, accept at 5 majors.

A tech pack for hoodie production needs no beauty, only the tolerance column, the consumption figure and the fastness floor. Those three are where the money leaks.
