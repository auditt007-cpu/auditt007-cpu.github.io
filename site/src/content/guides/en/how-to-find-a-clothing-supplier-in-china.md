## The direct answer

Through the boring route, and then through qualification. Nobody's first supplier was a lucky message. The channels below are ranked by what they convert into, not by how many results they return. For apparel, and especially for streetwear, the working gravity is Guangzhou: knitting mills, the Zhongda fabric market, wash houses, trim suppliers and sewing shops all sit inside a 30 km radius, which is why clothing manufacturers in Guangzhou China can hold 100-piece minimums that nobody in Vietnam will touch.

The shisanhang wholesale sourcing agent route is the most underrated entry: a two-day walk through the wholesale blocks plus the Zhanxi tailoring buildings produces 8 to 12 workshops that will take a 100-piece cut-make-trim order, and they are reachable by metro rather than by a translator on a call.

## Qualification sequence, in this order

1. Shortlist 12 names from at most two channels. More channels means shallower files.
2. Pull the business licence and read the scope of operations. It must include garment design, manufacturing or processing; "wholesale and retail" alone means you are talking to a reseller.
3. Check insured employee headcount on the public registry. A real Guangzhou sewing operation shows 30-200 people on social insurance. Zero to five means a shell, no matter how good the video tour looks.
4. Ask each candidate to name three styles they currently produce and buy one of them at retail price. Wear it, wash it three times, measure it against their own stated tolerances.
5. Request a gate price for one fixed construction — 320 GSM brushed fleece hoodie, garment dyed, 504 four-thread overlock, 1 cm seam allowance — so quotes become comparable.
6. Audit remotely or in person: count sewing machines, check whether the wash house is on site or subcontracted, ask how many dedicated QC heads they run and what they measure.
7. Place a paid sample at your own spec, RMB 250-500 per hoodie. Never accept a free sample; free samples are cut from their best stock and are not evidence of your bulk.
8. Trial 100-200 pieces with the inspection clause in the order before you sign anything larger.
9. Sign the NNN only when you hand over the graded nest, not when you first say hello.
10. Decide within 60 days. If they delivered the trial clean, put your second style with them; if they missed the window, move and keep the runner-up warm.

## Terms that survive the first bad batch

Pay 30% deposit and 70% against a passed pre-shipment inspection, always by wire to a company account whose name matches the licence. A personal account, or an account in a third company's name, is the single loudest fraud signal in this trade and it is not negotiable. Write into the purchase order: AQL 2.5 major and 4.0 minor at inspection level II, size tolerance plus or minus 0.5 cm on chest, a delivery window with a 0.5% per day delay penalty capped at 5%, and who pays rework. On quality control china manufacturing, the clause that matters is the one that delays your payment, because that is the one the factory reads.

A guangzhou streetwear clothing manufacturer that passes this sequence is usually a 40-120 person shop in Panyu or Zengcheng, 30 to 60 minutes from the city centre, and a single two-day trip covers fabric at Zhongda, wholesale at Shisanhang and CMT capacity at Zhanxi inside 12 km of each other. Sample parcels out to Europe or the US cost USD 45-70 for a kilogram and land in 4-6 days. That density is the entire reason to source here rather than elsewhere, and it is also the reason a bad supplier choice compounds so fast: everything they touch is three markets away from being replaced.
