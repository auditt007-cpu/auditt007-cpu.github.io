Finding a reliable apparel supplier in China, especially from Guangzhou, requires a structured approach focused on verification and clear communication. As a sourcing agent here, my process is built on direct market access and established relationships, not just online searches. The key is understanding the specific capabilities and limitations of different manufacturing hubs and individual factories.

## Sourcing Strategy and Execution

My approach to finding suppliers is systematic, combining market intelligence with direct factory engagement. It's about matching your brand's specific needs—fabric, style, volume, and price point—with the right production partner.

### Practical Steps to Supplier Identification

1.  **Define Your Product Specifications:** Before any search, have clear tech packs, material specifications (e.g., 180 GSM single jersey cotton, 340 GSM 10oz denim), and target price points. Without this, you cannot get accurate quotes or assess factory suitability.
2.  **Market Immersion (Guangzhou Specific):** For general apparel, I start with markets like Shisanhang (十三行) for fast fashion and volume, or Shahe (沙河) for denim and casual wear. These markets are not just for buying off-the-shelf; they are hubs where factory representatives showcase their capabilities and connect with buyers. For higher-end or specialized items, direct factory visits are essential.
3.  **Initial Supplier Vetting:** From market contacts or referrals, I identify potential factories. This involves checking their primary product categories, production capacity, and minimum order quantities (MOQs). A factory specializing in knitwear will not be suitable for woven jackets.
4.  **Request for Quotation (RFQ) and Sample Development:** Send detailed tech packs to 3-5 pre-vetted suppliers. Compare their initial quotes (FOB Guangzhou or Shenzhen) and lead times. Proceed with 2-3 for sample development. This is where you assess their understanding of your design and their sample-making quality.
5.  **Factory Audits and Capability Assessment:** Once samples are approved, I conduct a physical factory audit. This includes verifying their production lines, machinery, worker skill levels, and adherence to any specific compliance requirements (e.g., BSCI, Sedex). This step is crucial for ensuring consistent quality and ethical production.
6.  **Negotiation and Contract Finalization:** Negotiate pricing, payment terms (typically 30% deposit, 70% balance on shipment), lead times, and quality control standards. Ensure all agreements are clearly documented in a purchase order.

## Guangzhou Wholesale Reality and Numbers

Guangzhou is a powerhouse for apparel manufacturing, offering a vast ecosystem of suppliers. Understanding the specific market dynamics and typical numbers is critical for any buyer.

For a basic cotton T-shirt (e.g., 180 GSM single jersey), a typical MOQ per style per color is 300 pieces. The unit price range for this at 300 pieces would be approximately $3.50 - $5.00 USD, depending on print complexity and finishing. For a hoodie (e.g., 320 GSM fleece), the MOQ is often 300-500 pieces per style per color, with unit prices ranging from $8.00 - $12.00 USD. Denim jeans (10oz denim, 340 GSM) typically require a MOQ of 500 pieces per wash, with unit prices from $15.00 - $25.00 USD. These prices are FOB Guangzhou.

Quality control is paramount. We implement multiple checkpoints throughout the production process. This includes initial fabric inspection, in-line inspections during cutting and sewing, and a final pre-shipment inspection. For the final inspection, we adhere to AQL (Acceptable Quality Limit) standards, typically AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects. This means that for a given lot size, a specific number of defective units will trigger a rejection of the entire batch. A thorough quality check in China is non-negotiable. Our quality inspector China team ensures these standards are met, providing detailed reports and photographic evidence at each stage. This rigorous quality inspection China process minimizes risks for overseas buyers.
